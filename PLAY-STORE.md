## English

**Title (max 30):** Atlas Merge: Merge & Explore

**Short description (max 80):**
Merge items, restore the map and complete your atlas. Works offline.

**Full description:**
Atlas Merge is a relaxing merge game where every discovery restores a little more of the world map.

🧭 HOW TO PLAY
Tap "Explore" to get items. Match two identical ones and merge them into something better: from backpack to compass, map to telescope, balloon to rocket… and all the way to the planet.

🗺️ RESTORE THE ATLAS
Your items earn coins even while you're away. Spend them to restore the map's 8 regions: coast, jungle, mountain, desert, glacier, archipelago, savanna and volcano.

📖 COLLECTIONS TO DISCOVER
Each new item joins your collection, and you always see the ❓ silhouette of the next one to discover. Re-editing the atlas unlocks new collections: Explorers, Creatures and Wonders.

⚡ BUILT FOR SHORT SESSIONS
• A helper that merges for you and improves over time
• Combos for fast merging
• Quests, daily gift and streaks
• Progress while you're away (up to 8 hours)
• Atlas pages: a permanent income bonus
• Works offline, no account, no ads

No sign-up. No personal data. Just you and your map.
